This update improves how inbound message data is stored and accessed.

## What is new

- Added dashboard links to download full inbound payloads and raw email content.

## Why this matters

- Faster support and debugging workflows when you need the original data.
- Easier auditing and incident investigation.
- Better long-term storage efficiency as message volume grows.

If your team reviews inbound webhook traffic regularly, this should make day-to-day troubleshooting much easier.
